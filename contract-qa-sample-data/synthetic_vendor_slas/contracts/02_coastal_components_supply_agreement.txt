COMPONENT SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Component Supply Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective June 15, 2025, between Northgate Distribution Co. ("Buyer") and Coastal Components Inc. ("Supplier").

1. PRODUCTS AND ORDERING
Supplier shall manufacture and supply electronic components as described in Exhibit A, pursuant to purchase orders accepted by Supplier in writing.

2. DELIVERY PERFORMANCE
Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to meet the delivery dates set forth in each purchase order. In the event of a delay attributable to Supplier, Buyer may, at its discretion, withhold a portion of payment reasonably related to the resulting impact on Buyer's operations, subject to good-faith discussion between the parties regarding the appropriate amount. Repeated or chronic delays that Supplier fails to remedy after reasonable notice may be treated as a material breach of this Agreement.

Invoices are payable Net 30 from the invoice date. Buyer may deduct any amounts properly withheld under Section 2 from the corresponding invoice.

4. QUALITY AND INSPECTION
Buyer may reject any shipment that does not conform to the specifications in Exhibit A within fifteen (15) days of receipt. Rejected goods shall be replaced by Supplier at no additional cost within a commercially reasonable time.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION
5.1 This Agreement commences on the effective date and continues for eighteen (18) months, renewing thereafter on a month-to-month basis unless either party provides thirty (30) days' notice of termination.

Supplier's liability for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages is excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law. Direct damages are capped at the greater of $250,000 or the fees paid under the purchase order giving rise to the claim.
