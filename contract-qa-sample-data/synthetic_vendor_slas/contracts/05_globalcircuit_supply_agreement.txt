SUPPLY AND INDEMNIFICATION AGREEMENT

This Supply and Indemnification Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of September 3, 2025, between Northgate Distribution Co. ("Buyer") and Global Circuit Supply Ltd. ("Supplier").

Supplier shall supply circuit board assemblies per Buyer's purchase orders and the specifications in Exhibit A.

2.1 Supplier shall deliver within the lead time confirmed on each purchase order. Late delivery of more than five (5) business days results in a penalty of 3% of the delayed order's value per week of delay, capped at 15% of the order value.

3.1 Supplier shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Buyer from any third-party claims arising from (a) Supplier's breach of the warranties in Section 5, (b) actual or alleged infringement of intellectual property rights by the supplied goods, or (c) bodily injury or property damage caused by a defect in the supplied goods.

4. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
4.1 Except for the indemnification obligations in Section 3, breaches of confidentiality, or claims arising from a party's gross negligence or willful misconduct — for which liability is uncapped — each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is capped at two (2) times the fees paid in the preceding twelve (12) months.
4.2 Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, except with respect to the uncapped categories in Section 4.1.

5. WARRANTY
Supplier warrants the goods will conform to specifications and be free of defects for eighteen (18) months from delivery.

6. INSURANCE
Supplier shall maintain product liability insurance of not less than $5,000,000 per occurrence and shall provide Buyer a certificate of insurance upon request.

7.2 Either party may terminate for an uncured material breach following thirty (30) days' written notice. Buyer may also terminate immediately if Supplier becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy protection.
